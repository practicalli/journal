## Careers

Initial interview with Utility Warehouse this week, with their recruiter.

The company is interesting, combining utilities (energy, broadband, mobile, insurance) to make it simpler to manage essential bills.

The role seems to be wider in scope that the average Engineering Manager role. For the first year it seems more like a tech lead whist engineering team are established. I assume the Engineering Manager duties would kick in later as the team grows.

The recruiter was open about the role and asked questions about how 'hands-on' I am. I am unsure if they were convinced, but seemed happy to pass on my application to the hiring manager.

They are looking for engineers with Go language experience. This made me curious as to how active the Golang community is (I did some work with the GoLang community back in 2016, but out of touch since then).

Debian Linux (stable) has a version of Golang released in February 2025. The latest version is 1.27 released August 2026.

Golang supports the latest two releases, so the Debian Linux package is not officially supported.
