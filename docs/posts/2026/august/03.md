I bought a large standing freezer just before the pandemic and it was useful for handling larger frozen food orders I had delivered from Ocado.

During the pandemic this was useful to limit my exposure to Covid.  I still suffered greatly from that virus but having to visit the supermarket more often would have exacerbated the issue (and at times it was just not possible to leave the house).

## Practicalli

Testing of the Megalinter custom flavors for Clojure and Zensical have been productive and successful.

The Clojure flavor was used to resolve issues in the [practicalli/clojure-through-code](https://github.com/practicalli/clojure-through-code){target=_blank}.

The Clojure flavor is running in around 1 minute, compared to 5-10 minutes that the Java flavor used to take.

Its time to migrate all Practicalli projects.
